Palette, second round

Three options built from B and C, none with more than four colours: paper, ink, an accent for actions, and one alert colour for things that need you. The screen under each is the same Today page, painted in that option.

B · Ink

White paper, near-black ink, deep blue for actions, burnt orange for alerts.

C · Clay

Three colours only. Cream paper, warm ink, and terracotta for both actions and alerts.

E · Paper

B and C together: cream paper, deep blue for actions, terracotta for alerts.

Greys, dividers and the calendar bars are the ink colour at reduced opacity, so each option really is three or four colours. Platform names are written out instead of coloured badges. The alert colour appears only when something needs you; everything else is ink on paper.
