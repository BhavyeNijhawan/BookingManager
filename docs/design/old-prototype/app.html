Atithi

Every booking from Airbnb, MakeMyTrip, Agoda and Booking.com in one phone app. One booking blocks the dates everywhere, guests get WhatsApp messages automatically, and pay-at-property bookings are verified before they can cost you a night.

How to demo:

  1. Start on Today. Two bookings need attention. Tap one.
  2. Open Calendar. Every colour is a platform. Striped means not yet verified.
  3. Tap the Demo button and run the MakeMyTrip pay-at-property story step by step.
  4. Open More › Caretaker view to see what Ramesh bhai sees.
