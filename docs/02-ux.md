# 02 · UX

How Atithi looks and behaves: tabs and screens, the words used for status, the "Paper" design system, the caretaker view, notifications, installation and accessibility. The prototype (`prototype/`) is the reference for layout and wording. Research 02 is the reference for patterns.

## 1. Principles

Adapted from research 02 (§5.4):

1. **Today answers "do I need to do anything?" in 3 seconds.** Attention first, then today, then the next two weeks.
2. **Every alert carries its fix.** One primary button per row. No dead-end notifications.
3. **Status is written in words.** Colour only adds emphasis, and only when Rohan must act.
4. **Money is always a number.** "₹9,800 due at property", never "partially paid".
5. **Sync is visible.** Show when each platform last synced and whether each night is closed on each platform.
6. **Automate by default, keep a human gate where trust matters.** Routine messages send themselves. Cancelling and releasing dates always needs Rohan's tap.
7. **The caretaker view needs zero training.** Hindi, big buttons, few words.
8. **WhatsApp and Call are one tap from every booking.**
9. **It works on a bad network.** Today and the next two weeks open offline. Changes made offline show as pending.
10. **Interrupt sparingly.** Tiers, quiet hours, a morning summary. Only real emergencies break through.

## 2. Where the design departs from research 02

Research 02 proposed coloured status chips (amber, green, blue, red), brand-tinted platform monograms (Ab, Bk, Ag, MMT, Dir) and a dark theme (§3.4). The client rejected that look as too busy and chose the "Paper" palette: four colours, status in words, platform names written out, no badges, no cards (prototype `styles.css`).

What stays from research 02:

- **Never colour alone** (WCAG 1.4.1). Every status is a written label, and the calendar uses line style (dashed, hatched), not just colour (§3.1).
- **Dashed means unverified** (§1.3, after Lodgify's dotted tentative bookings).
- **Per-platform block state** is the differentiator (§3.5). It appears as "On every platform" in booking detail and "N of 4 closed".
- **The alert colour is reserved** for things that need Rohan (§3.1 rule 5).

What changes:

| Research 02 | Atithi |
|---|---|
| Status chips with tinted backgrounds | A status sentence under the row: "Not verified yet · ₹14,500 due at property" |
| Channel monograms (Ab, Bk, MMT) | Platform names in full: "Airbnb", "Booking.com", "MakeMyTrip" |
| Floating action button | A text button "Add" at the top right of Calendar and Bookings |
| Card per attention item | A row with hairline dividers |
| "Sab theek hai" empty state with a tick mark | "Nothing needs you right now" |
| Light and dark themes | Light only for MVP. Dark can come later if Rohan asks. |

## 3. Information architecture

Five tabs, labels always visible (research 02, §2.1 and §5.1). Actions are never tabs.

| Tab | Question it answers | Contents | Badge |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Today** | Does anything need me? What is happening today? | Needs your attention · Today (leaving, arriving, staying) · Next two weeks (timeline) · This month's numbers · Platforms one-liner | A dot in the alert colour when something needs action |
| **Calendar** | What is booked or free, and is it closed everywhere? | Two-week timeline for both units · Month grid per unit · Day sheet | None |
| **Bookings** | Find any booking | Text tabs: Needs action · Upcoming · Staying · Past · Cancelled · platform filter · search by name, phone or platform reference (research 02, §5.1) | None |
| **Messages** | What did guests say, and what will Atithi send next? | Chats (WhatsApp threads) · Going out next (scheduled sends, each skippable) · link to Automations | Unread count, in ink (not the alert colour) |
| **More** | Everything else | Platforms and sync · Automations · Caretaker view · Reports · Payments · Property, units and people · Notifications · Install app · Help | None |

There is no bell icon. Today is the notification centre (research 02, §5.1).

The top bar shows a small grey line (the date, or the property name) above a large title. A text button can sit at the right ("Add", "Booking", "Automations"). Detail screens add a back arrow.

## 4. Screens

### 4.1 Today

- **Purpose:** answer "do I need to do anything?" and show the day.
- **Needs your attention:** one row per booking that needs Rohan. Line 1: guest name and amount. Line 2: unit, dates, nights, platform and pay mode. Line 3, in the alert colour: why it needs him ("WhatsApp sent 2 hours ago, no reply yet. The dates are held on every platform meanwhile."). Below: one primary button ("Remind on WhatsApp", "Resend payment link", "Cancel and release dates") and "Call". Conflicts and sync failures appear here too.
- **Empty state:** "Nothing needs you right now" with "Every booking is verified and paid where it should be."
- **Today:** who leaves (with time), who arrives, who is staying, and the amount to collect. A grey note says whether Ramesh bhai got and answered his briefing.
- **Next two weeks:** the timeline (§6.4), with a link to the full calendar.
- **This month:** three numbers: bookings value, occupancy, fake bookings caught (with the value of nights freed). Link to Reports.
- **Platforms:** one sentence on sync freshness, for example "MakeMyTrip synced at 10:32 AM; one pay-at-property booking is waiting for the guest." Link to Platforms and sync.

### 4.2 Calendar

- **Two-week timeline:** both units as rows, starting yesterday. Today's date is circled in ink. Bars carry the guest's first name when there is room.
- **Month grid:** per unit, chosen by text tabs. Monday first. Past days in grey.
- **Legend:** Confirmed · Not verified yet · Likely fake · Blocked.
- **Day sheet** (tap a date): one row per unit. A booked unit shows the booking and "Blocked on Booking.com, Agoda, MakeMyTrip". A blocked unit shows the reason and "Unblock". An open unit shows "Bookable on Airbnb, Booking.com, Agoda and MakeMyTrip" and "Block". Bottom button: "Add a direct booking for this night".
- **Add** (top right): Direct booking, or Block dates.

### 4.3 Bookings

- Text tabs with a count on "Needs action".
- A plain platform filter (a dressed-down native select).
- Rows: name and amount, then dates, nights, unit and platform, then the status sentence.
- Search is not in the prototype yet. Add it in MVP: name, phone or platform reference.

### 4.4 Booking detail

Top to bottom:

1. **Title:** guest name, with the platform and its reference above.
2. **Head:** dates and unit (large); nights, guests and pay mode; the status sentence ("Not verified yet. ₹14,500 due at property."); the actions for this status (§5.3) plus Call and WhatsApp, and Cancel where allowed.
3. **Details:** check-in day and time, checkout, total, payout or amount to collect, phone, platform reference.
4. **On every platform:** one row per platform: "Source" for the platform the booking came from, "Blocked", "Updating" (with the push time), or "Open" after a cancellation. A count on the right: "3 of 4 closed". For MakeMyTrip bookings, a link "Open on InGo-MMT".
5. **Notes** (owner and caretaker).
6. **What happened:** a dated history. Upcoming steps start with "Next:". Critical steps are in the alert colour.
7. **Caretaker:** when Ramesh bhai was or will be briefed.

### 4.5 Messages

- **Chats:** one row per guest, newest first. Line 2 is the last message. Line 3 is the platform, dates and unit. The caretaker's thread is included and marked "Caretaker · daily briefing".
- **Thread:** day separators; outgoing messages on a light fill; incoming ones outlined. Messages sent by an automation are labelled "Sent automatically". Quick-reply buttons show as the guest sees them. A compose bar is pinned above the tab bar. "Booking" at the top right opens the booking.
- **Going out next:** every scheduled send with its time, guest and rule, each with "Skip". Replying by hand drops a pending reminder (research 02, §1.6, after Hospitable).
- In MVP the thread is read-mostly. Free-text replies need the 24-hour window (04-integrations §5.4), so outside the window the compose bar offers templates only. The full two-way inbox is v1.

### 4.6 Automations

- **List:** one row per rule: name, "when → then", "Ran 14 times in 30 days", and an on/off switch. Rules that are part of how Atithi works (blocking dates everywhere) are marked "built in".
- **Rule detail:** When · Only if · Then · Status, a preview of the guest message with the fill-in fields in the accent colour, "Edit message", "Send me a test", and recent runs.
- MVP ships fixed recipes with switches and a few parameters. A form-based editor comes in v1 (research 03, §E.1).

## 5. Status language

### 5.1 Rule

The status is always a word or a short sentence. The alert colour (`--alert`) is added only when Rohan must act. Everything else is ink or grey.

### 5.2 Booking statuses

Status keys and owner wording come from the prototype (`STATUS` in `app.js`). Caretaker wording in Roman Hinglish comes from research 02 (§3.2). Devanagari strings need checking with Ramesh bhai before launch.

| Key | Owner sees | Caretaker sees (Hinglish) | Colour | Holds the nights? | Calendar bar |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `unverified` | Not verified yet | Confirm karna hai | Alert | Yes | Dashed outline in the alert colour, no fill |
| `verified` | Guest said yes (only while an advance is asked and unpaid) | Confirm karna hai | Alert | Yes | Dashed, as above |
| `flagged` | No reply in 24 hours · likely fake | Shak hai | Alert, weight 500 | Yes, until Rohan acts | Light alert fill with a solid alert outline |
| `confirmed` | Confirmed | Pakka | Ink | Yes | Solid grey fill |
| `in_house` | Staying now | Ruke hue | Ink | Yes | Solid grey fill |
| `checked_out` | Checked out | Chale gaye | Grey | Past nights only | Faint fill, grey text |
| `cancelled` | Cancelled | Cancel | Grey | No | Not drawn |
| `no_show` | No-show | Nahi aaye | Grey (alert only while a Booking.com report is due) | No | Not drawn |
| `tentative` | Details pending (iCal fallback only) | Not shown | Grey | Yes | Dashed outline in grey |
| Conflict | Double booked | Not shown | Alert, weight 500 | Both kept | Alert outline on both bars and a banner on Today |
| Block | Blocked · reason | Band | Grey | Yes | Diagonal hatch |

With the MakeMyTrip advance off (the default), a "yes" moves a booking straight from `unverified` to `confirmed`, and `verified` never appears. `verified` exists only while an advance is requested and unpaid. `tentative` exists only if Atithi falls back to iCal (04-integrations §8).

## 6. Design system: "Paper"

### 6.1 Colour tokens

Four colours. Every grey is the ink at reduced opacity (prototype `styles.css`, lines 1–20).

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--paper` | `#f8f6f1` | Background everywhere, including sheets and the tab bar |
| `--ink` | `#1e1d1a` | Text, active tab, switches when on, today's date marker |
| `--accent` | `#2e4a6b` | Actions only: primary buttons, links, text buttons in the top bar, fill-in fields in message previews |
| `--alert` | `#b4573c` | Only for things that need the owner: the attention label and dot, unverified and flagged status lines, the conflict outline, danger buttons |
| `--ink-2` | ink at 58% | Secondary text: second lines, labels, hints |
| `--ink-3` | ink at 38% | Faint text: timestamps, inactive tab labels, past days |
| `--line` | ink at 10% | Hairline dividers |
| `--line-2` | ink at 20% | Button and input borders, the hatch pattern |
| `--fill` | ink at 13% | Confirmed booking bars |
| `--fill-2` | ink at 6% | Checked-out bars, outgoing message bubbles |
| `--fill-3` | ink at 30% | Report bars |
| `--alert-fill` | alert at 12% | The likely-fake bar |
| `--scrim` | ink at 35% | Behind sheets |
| `--shadow` | `0 8px 28px` ink at 14% | Toasts only |

Platform brand colours are never used. Section 11 lists contrast fixes to agree with the client.

### 6.2 Type

Poppins 400 and 500 only. No bold, no uppercase labels, no letter-spaced captions.

| Role | Size / line height | Weight |
|---|---|---|

| Booking dates (detail head) | 18 px / 1.3 | 500 |
| Sheet title | 17 px | 500 |
| Month title | 16 px | 500 |
| Row line 1 (name) | 14 px / 1.35 | 500 |
| Body | 14 px / 1.5 | 400 |
| Row meta (amount, time) | 13 px, tabular figures | 400 |
| Row lines 2 and 3, hints | 12.5 px / 1.45 | 400 |
| Section label, top-bar line | 12 px | 400 |
| Numbers block value | 19 px, tabular figures | 500 |

Numbers use tabular figures so amounts line up.

**Font loading.** The prototype loads Poppins from Google Fonts. In production, self-host two weights with `next/font`, subset to what is used. Poppins may include Devanagari glyphs itself: **verify** in the Google Fonts subset before adding a second font.

| Component | Rules |

One per row or screen. **Quiet** (default): transparent with a `--line-2` border. **Danger:** alert fill, paper text, only for destructive actions ("Cancel and release dates"). **Big:** 50 px tall, for the caretaker and full-width actions. **Link:** accent text, underlined. |

### 6.5 Formats

From research 02 (§4.4):

- **Money:** `Intl.NumberFormat('en-IN')` with a ₹ prefix and no paise: ₹12,500, ₹1,25,000. Compact forms only in tight calendar cells: ₹12.5k, ₹1.2L.
- **Dates:** "Sun, 11 Oct". Ranges "14–16 Oct"; across months "30 Oct – 2 Nov". The year only when it is not this year. Never numeric dates like 10/11.
- **Times:** 12-hour, "1 PM", "2:15 PM". Relative helpers: "2 min ago", "Tomorrow".
- **Phones:** stored as `+919876543210`, shown as `+91 98765 43210`. Masked where the viewer should not see the full number: `+91 98••• ••210`.
- **Platform names** in full: Airbnb, Booking.com, Agoda, MakeMyTrip (Goibibo bookings say "Goibibo"), Direct.

## 7. Caretaker view

Ramesh bhai is not comfortable with technology. The view is built for him first and for Rohan never.

**Access**

- A link sent on WhatsApp opens the view on his phone. A 4-digit PIN reopens it. No email, no password, no account to set up (research 02, §1.7 and §5.1).
- The link can be revoked from More › Property, units and people.

**Language**

- Hindi by default, as in the prototype (Devanagari). A toggle offers Hinglish in Roman script and English.
- Research 02 (§4.1) recommends Hinglish as the default because it matches how caretakers type on WhatsApp. Ask Ramesh bhai which he reads faster (01-product §9, question 7).
- Numbers and ₹ stay in Western digits.

**Three things at most**

The screen answers three questions and nothing else:

1. **Who leaves today** (चेक-आउट), with the time.
2. **Who comes today** (चेक-इन), with the number of people and the money to collect.
3. **What to do** (आज के काम): cleaning, room ready.

Tomorrow's arrivals sit below in one grey line. Nothing else: no revenue, no platforms, no settings.

**Rules**

- Text at least 16 px. Names 17 px. Buttons at least 48 px tall (research 02, §4.1; prototype uses 48–50 px).
- **One big button per row:** गेस्ट आ गए (guest arrived), गेस्ट चले गए (guest left), काम पूरा (done). Secondary actions sit beside it as a quiet button (कॉल).
- **"मालिक को कॉल करें" (call the owner)** is always at the top, full width.
- Guest phone numbers are masked. Calling a guest goes through the masked number where the platform provides one.
- The only money shown is the amount to collect at check-in.
- Every tap works offline and says so: "Save ho gaya, net aane par bhej denge" (research 02, §4.1). The mark syncs when the network returns (research 03, §B.2).
- Finished items collapse to "हो गया" (done).
- Voice notes from Rohan are a later addition (research 02, §4.1, after MakeMyTrip's "How to Reach" voice notes).

**WhatsApp first.** Before the view exists (MVP), Ramesh bhai gets the same content as a Hindi WhatsApp message at 8 AM (prototype rule "Morning briefing for Ramesh bhai"). An 8 PM "kal" (tomorrow) preview is optional (research 02, §2.4). This works even if he never opens the app.

## 8. Notifications

### 8.1 Tiers

Adapted from research 02 (§2.4) and the prototype's notification settings:

| Tier | Events | Delivery | During quiet hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 Urgent** | Possible double booking · a platform still open after the push window · channel manager silent for 60 minutes · a booking not verified with check-in less than 24 hours away · guest not arrived 2 hours after check-in time (v1, once the caretaker marks arrivals) | Push **and** WhatsApp to Rohan | Sent anyway |
| **P2 Act today** | Pay-at-property booking to verify · booking flagged as likely fake · guest tapped Cancel · cancellation from a platform · email waiting in the review queue · Booking.com phone number to copy | Push. The likely-fake flag also goes to WhatsApp (research 03, §E.1). | Held until quiet hours end |
| **P3 Info** | New prepaid booking · guest said yes · advance paid · caretaker marked a guest arrived or a room ready · new guest WhatsApp message | Push, grouped per booking ("Priya Sharma · 3 updates") | Held |
| **Digest** | Morning summary for Rohan at 8 AM (off by default in the prototype) · caretaker briefing at 8 AM · optional caretaker preview at 8 PM | WhatsApp, plus push for Rohan | Scheduled |

Each event type has its own switch in More › Notifications.

### 8.2 Quiet hours

- **Owner alerts:** default 10 PM to 7 AM (research 02, §2.4). The prototype's settings screen says 11 PM to 7 AM. Make it a setting and agree the default with Rohan. Only P1 comes through.
- **Guest messages:** never sent between 9:30 PM and 8 AM. They wait for the morning (research 03, §E.2).

### 8.3 Content

- Ask for push permission only after a tap, never on first launch. Explain first: "Get an alert when a booking arrives or a guest needs checking" (research 02, §2.4).
- No guest details on the lock screen: "New MakeMyTrip booking · tap to review" (research 03, §B.3).
- Bundle bursts per booking instead of sending five pushes (research 02, §2.4).
- Every push opens the screen where the fix is.

## 9. Installing the app (PWA)

**Android (Chrome).** Catch `beforeinstallprompt`, hide the browser's mini-infobar, and offer "Install app" in context: after the first useful action (for example, after Beds24 is connected), in More, or as a dismissible row on Today. Remember a dismissal (research 02, §2.5).

**iPhone.** There is no install prompt. Show our own sheet with the steps: Share › Add to Home Screen. Web push works only for apps added to the Home Screen on iOS 16.4 or later, and permission must follow a tap (research 03, §B.3). So on iPhone, onboarding asks Rohan to install first and allow notifications second. iOS 26 opens Home Screen sites as web apps by default but still has no automatic prompt (research 03, §B.3). Use Declarative Web Push (iOS 18.4+) so notifications display without waking the service worker (research 03, §B.3).

**Badge.** Use the Badging API to put the needs-attention count on the app icon where supported (iOS 16.4+ Home Screen apps, desktop Chromium). Android launchers show dots instead, so feature-detect (research 03, §B.4).

**Backup channel.** Web push can fail silently: permission revoked, app not installed on iPhone, battery savers. Every P1 alert, and the likely-fake flag, also goes to Rohan on WhatsApp (research 02, §2.4; research 03, §B.3).

**Never rely on** Periodic Background Sync. It is Chromium-only and runs when the browser chooses. All scheduling happens on the server (research 03, §B.4).

## 10. Network, loading and feedback

From research 02 (§2.3) and research 03 (§B.2):

- **Freshness:** show "Updated 2 min ago" so manual refresh is rarely needed. Pull-to-refresh is built in-app with `overscroll-behavior`.
- **Loading:** nothing for under 1 second; a spinner for 2–10 seconds; a skeleton for full-page loads.
- **Offline:** a banner (Offline / Syncing), the queue of pending changes, and "3 changes failed · Retry". Today, the next 14 days of arrivals and departures, and the caretaker's list are cached for offline reading.
- **What works offline:** only low-risk marks: guest arrived, guest left, task done, checklist ticks. Creating or changing a booking needs a connection, because the overlap check runs on the server.
- **Pending is never shown as saved.** Money and status changes show as pending until the server confirms.
- **Undo:** blocks and direct bookings offer a short undo in the toast.
- **Haptics:** Android only (`navigator.vibrate`), as an extra, never as the only feedback.

## 11. Accessibility

### 11.1 Contrast

Measured against `--paper` with the WCAG formula (computed for this plan from the token values in §6.1):

| Pair | Ratio | WCAG AA for small text (4.5:1) |
|---|---|---|
| Ink on paper | 15.6 : 1 | Pass |
| Accent on paper, and paper on accent | 8.4 : 1 | Pass |
| `--ink-2` (58%) on paper | about 4.1 : 1 | **Fail** |
| `--ink-3` (38%) on paper | about 2.3 : 1 | **Fail** |
| Alert on paper, and paper on alert | 4.45 : 1 | **Just under** (passes for text at 18.7 px bold or 24 px and above) |
| Alert on the likely-fake bar fill | about 3.8 : 1 | **Fail** for the 11 px bar text |
| `--line-2` border on paper | 1.5 : 1 | Below the 3:1 non-text guideline (1.4.11) for component borders |

Proposed fixes, for the client to approve (he picks colours himself):

- `--ink-2` from 58% to about 65% (5.1 : 1). 62% is the minimum for 4.5 : 1.
- `--ink-3` only for things that need not be read (decoration, disabled), or raised to 62% where it carries text such as timestamps and inactive tab labels.
- A slightly darker alert for text, for example `#a65035` (about 5.1 : 1), keeping `#b4573c` for fills and outlines.
- Quiet buttons are identified by their text label, which passes. Keep labels on every button so the faint border is never the only cue.

### 11.2 Other rules

- **Never colour alone.** Status is always written. Calendar states also differ by line style (solid, dashed, hatched), and every bar has an `aria-label` with the guest, dates and status (prototype already does this).
- **Tap targets:** at least 44 px for the owner's buttons (prototype buttons are 38 px tall; raise them) and at least 48 px for the caretaker. Rows are full-width targets.
- **Small text:** tab-bar labels (10.5 px) and timeline text (11 px) are at the lower limit. Do not go smaller, and support the browser's text-size setting by sizing in `rem`.
- **Semantics:** the prototype marks text tabs as `role="tab"` with `aria-pressed`. Use `aria-selected` for tabs, or make them plain toggle buttons with `aria-pressed`. Keep `aria-current="page"` on the tab bar, `role="switch"` with `aria-checked` on switches, and `role="dialog"` with `aria-modal` on sheets.
- **Focus:** move focus into a sheet when it opens, keep it there, and return it to the button that opened it. The prototype does not do this yet.
- **Language:** mark Hindi text with `lang="hi"` so screen readers switch voice.
- **Motion:** respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for the sheet slide and toast fade.
- **Live updates:** toasts use `aria-live="polite"`. Conflict banners should be announced assertively.
